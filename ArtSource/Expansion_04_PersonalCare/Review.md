# Visual review

Iteration 01: inspected the six-item render. The deodorant cap had an unintended opening on top. Tissue geometry was too narrow to read, and its thickness direction needed correcting. Blister and bottle silhouettes matched the established gentle-facet style.

Iteration 02: reversed the cap cavity direction to give it a closed domed roof; added crimp folds, bristle rows, cap hinge/seam and the twist base. Corrected paper thickness and added a third sheet. Reviewed the render and found coplanar surfaces at the twist base and edge-on paper orientation.

Iteration 03: made the twist base slightly wider to prevent coplanar overlap. Turned and widened the paper sheets into readable folds facing the game camera. Inspected the final render: closed dome, clean base band and three visible tissue sheets. All six GLBs pass the budget and transform audit.

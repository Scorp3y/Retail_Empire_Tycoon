# Visual review

Iteration 01: inspected empty and stocked renders. Large homeware and diaper packs fit without scaling. Cubby dividers were coplanar with horizontal board fronts, producing dark joints. Lower stationery pocket needed a shorter front lip for notebook readability.

Iteration 02: recessed divider fronts, lowered pocket lips, added stationery compartment dividers, upper cubby lining and restrained frame/header details. Inspected both final renders: clean cubby joints, readable stepped compartments, open homeware frame and appropriately spaced baby goods. All six cubby fronts were independently ray-tested open. Exported hierarchy, constant board intervals, material counts, slot coordinates and full budgets including colliders pass.

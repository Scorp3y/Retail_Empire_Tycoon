# Visual review

Iteration 01: inspected empty and stocked renders. Pegboard marks remain lightweight geometry rather than textures. Broom handles needed attachment to the actual highest part of the product instead of its AABB centre; the leaning handle was otherwise offset from the hook.

Iteration 02: used the top-vertex attachment centre, added panel trims, rack brace and garden back accent. Inspected both final renders: rods meet hook tips, buckets stand on the lower board, no rear panel on the cleaning rack, and real gaps remain between garden planks. All floor anchors pass surface ray tests; hanging anchors pass nearest-geometry tests. All GLBs pass exported hierarchy, collider and budget checks.

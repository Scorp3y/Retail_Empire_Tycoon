# Visual review

Reviewed actual previews 01, 02 and 03. The first pass already had visible real cavities and appropriately chunky walls; the pan handle sat too close to the saucepan handle in the row, and the plate's green rim was too thin.

Second pass rotated the pan, broadened ceramic colour bands and defined the translucent lid edge. Third pass slightly thickened the mug handle, refined the plate well and bowl floor, added a grey geometric pan rim, a saucepan knob collar and the container's thumb latch. Final render accepted: silhouettes are distinct, cavities remain visible, the translucent container has readable wall/lid boundaries and 0.25 alpha.

Cup/pot C-handles attach at their ends without running a closed oval through the inner cavity. The pan hanging loop is true tube geometry with an open centre. Vessel profiles run down the inner wall to an actual inner floor, maintaining nonzero wall and bottom thickness.

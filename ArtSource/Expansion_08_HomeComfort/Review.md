# Visual review

Iteration 01: inspected candle, lamp, clock, frame, cushion and blanket. The frame borders overlapped on a common front plane, creating dark joints. Cushion needed an actual centre indentation and a visible perimeter seam. Blanket topology initially exceeded its budget; changed to intentionally larger geometric checks while retaining the physical folds and fringe.

Iteration 02: removed border overlap, added foreground hill and lamp plug, indented the cushion around its button and followed its actual equator with the seam. Added wax pool, clock pivot and one fringe strand. Inspected final render: clean frame joints, readable clock ticks/hands, clearly hollow lampshade, layered checked textile and soft cushion silhouette. All six exports pass their budgets. SourceAudit.json additionally verifies the open shade's closed wall volume and the flame's emissive factor in the exported GLB.

# Visual review

Iteration 01: checked the row render. Loaf scoring incorrectly depressed the end caps, producing a pointed artefact. The pastry silhouettes, ring opening and tart contents were readable. Icing geometry initially exceeded the triangle budget and was rebuilt with a gentler path rather than decimated.

Iteration 02: isolated the score deformation from loaf end caps; added more sesame seeds, gingerbread icing cuffs, kiwi seeds and an eclair cream seam. Inspected the final render: rounded loaf ends, readable spiral, fruit pieces and filling. All six exports pass the geometry audit. Colours retain the subdued established palette; the preview is deliberately less saturated than the illustrated proposal.

# Visual review

Iteration 01: inspected empty and stocked renders. Coincident base/post and bin rim faces caused dark patches. Bin floor was too low for small packaged goods to be visible or easily reached.

Iteration 02: corrected base widths and raised the working bin floor; added useful base/header accents. Inspection found two remaining coplanar joints at the island crossbar and outer bin rim.

Iteration 03: recessed crossbar depth and widened outer rim surfaces by a few millimetres. Inspected both final renders: clean joints, clear surface levels, open island and visible rear bin products. All slots are present in exported GLBs and all four hierarchy/material/geometry audits pass. Goods were imported from existing GLBs without rescaling and are absent from the exported fixtures.

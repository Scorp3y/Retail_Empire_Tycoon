# Visual review

Reviewed native previews 01, 02 and 03. First pass had partially hidden bear paw pads and doll flower. Projected those details in front of their supporting surfaces, added belly patch and dress ribbon, offset the brick stack to expose studs, and enlarged the exposed tree trunk on the puzzle picture.

Third pass added the bear's geometric smile, doll fringe/pigtail ties, central window pillars, real tiny ball valve and the full puzzle rim. Final render accepted: all six silhouettes are distinct; faces and toy details read from the shared game camera.

Football is a mathematically truncated icosahedron: 12 black pentagons and 20 cream hexagons. The low triangle count follows this requested topology rather than removal of patches or replacement by a texture. All 16 brick studs are real geometry.

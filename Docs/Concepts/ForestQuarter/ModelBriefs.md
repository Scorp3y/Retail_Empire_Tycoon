# English native 3D generation briefs

Implemented by the project's deterministic Unity mesh generator, not an external text-to-3D service. Dimensions are metres. The approved concept is art direction, not a pixel-exact construction drawing.

## Architecture

Create a restrained contemporary European woodland neighbourhood compatible with the existing low-poly town. Cream brick, sparse mortar joints, warm vertical timber accent bays, sage window frames, graphite standing-seam gable roofs, shallow foundations, entrance canopies, slim rainwater pipes, layered window sills, practical projecting balconies with metal rails and planted boxes. Avoid skyscrapers, blank orange boxes, excessive surface noise and facades without depth. Ground-centred pivots, sensible collision envelopes, shared local palette.

- CourtyardHouse: three storeys, 22 by 12 metre footprint, approximately 12.8 metre roof height. Framed windows on all elevations, central canopied entrance, projecting planted balconies.
- PassageHouse: matching architecture with a 4.4 metre wide and 3.5 metre high open passage. Separate physical wings and lintel; never block the passage with a whole-building collider.
- CafeCorner / MarketCorner: matching mixed-use architecture, large ground-floor windows, awnings, English signs CAFE / LOCAL MARKET. Closed interiors for the current driving-game scope.
- TownhouseA / TownhouseB: two storeys, 8.4 by 10 metre footprint, assembled into attached pairs with shared party walls and non-overlapping roofs. Alternating timber accent sides, layered windows, graphite gable roof, entrance canopy, side windows, rear garden.
- CommunityPavilion: open post-and-beam timber shelter, slatted rear screen, graphite roof, English fascia COMMUNITY HUB. Clear entrance and seating space.

## Public space kit

Match dark metal and warm timber across benches, bins, pergolas, cycle shelters and single-arm lamps. Make details readable at street scale. Include stone-edged tree planters, PARCELS lockers with individual compartments, enclosed waste storage, screened utility cabinets, mailboxes, a bus shelter labelled FOREST QUARTER, cycle hoops, cafe tables and a menu board.

Create a timber play tower with sage slide, static two-seat swing and timber-edged sandpit. Group them on a defined surface outside vehicle routes. Group mixed low-poly planting and flower strips at edges; do not scatter identical objects uniformly across lawns.

## Placement

Continue the road beyond the single northern crossing. Keep the accepted railway, crossing and old city unchanged. Two courtyard groups, a quiet townhouse edge, gateway square, community park and woodland trail. Clear whole trees only in required development envelopes; retain perimeter forest. Eight-metre carriageways, three-metre pavement strips, small parking pockets, flush driving seams and non-colliding road paint. All new model lettering in English.
